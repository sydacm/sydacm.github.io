# Who we are

Sydney Anglicans Chinese Ministry (SYDACM) serves the Chinese-speaking churches and congregations of the Anglican Diocese of Sydney. We exist so that Chinese people across Sydney may hear the gospel of Jesus Christ in their heart language, be joined to a local church, and grow as disciples who make disciples.

# What we do

- **Connect** newcomers and enquirers with a local Anglican church that holds services in Mandarin or Cantonese, or offers translation.
- **Equip** ministers and congregations with Chinese-language resources, including services for baptism, confirmation, Holy Communion, marriage and funerals.
- **Pioneer** new Chinese-language gospel work in parts of Sydney where there is none yet.
- **Gather** Chinese-speaking ministers and leaders for fellowship, prayer and encouragement.

# What we believe

We stand with the Anglican Diocese of Sydney in the historic, Reformed, evangelical faith: the Bible is God's word and our final authority; we are saved by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone; and the risen Lord Jesus is building his church.

> Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path. — Psalm 119:105

===== 中文 =====

# 我们是谁

悉尼圣公会华文事工（SYDACM）服侍悉尼圣公会教区内的华语教会及会众。我们盼望悉尼的华人能以母语听闻耶稣基督的福音，归入地方教会，并成长为使人作门徒的门徒。

# 我们做什么

- **联结**：帮助新朋友和慕道者找到设有国语、粤语崇拜或提供翻译的圣公会教会。
- **装备**：为牧者和会众提供中文资源，包括洗礼、坚信礼、圣餐、婚礼及丧礼的礼文。
- **开拓**：在悉尼尚未有华语福音工作的地区开展新的事工。
- **团聚**：让华语牧者与领袖彼此团契、代祷和鼓励。

# 我们的信仰

我们与悉尼圣公会教区一同持守历史性的、归正的福音派信仰：圣经是神的话语，是我们最终的权威；我们得救唯独本乎恩、唯独藉着信、唯独在基督里；复活的主耶稣正在建立祂的教会。

> 你的话是我脚前的灯，是我路上的光。——诗篇 119:105
