# Welcome

Sydney Anglicans Chinese Ministry pioneers Chinese-language gospel work and strengthens ministry among Chinese communities across Sydney.

Churches across the Diocese hold Sunday services in Mandarin or Cantonese, or offer translation in their English services. Wherever you live in Sydney, we would love to help you find a church where the Bible is taught and Christ is proclaimed.

# 欢迎

悉尼圣公会华文事工致力开拓中文福音工作，并加强悉尼各社区的华人事工。

悉尼教区内多间教会设有国语或粤语主日崇拜，或在英语崇拜中提供翻译。无论你住在悉尼哪一区，我们都乐意帮助你找到一间忠于圣经教导、高举基督的教会。
